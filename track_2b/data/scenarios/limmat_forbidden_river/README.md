# Limmat — The Forbidden River

An English adventure adapted from the supplied Limmat campaign: an ordinary
arrival in Zurich opens onto two magical universities, a treaty of concealment,
a neutral student network and a river that reflects incompatible dawns.

All **16 named source characters** retain their identities and original offices.
They appear under romanized names, with their original names as aliases. The
Protagonist remains an unnamed new student that the player can rename. The
Second Investigator is an additional optional PC for demonstrating a second team.
Li's Home remains at Freiestrasse 14 in the story.

This is fictional campaign material. The magical roles, hidden treaty, secret
railway service and new river investigation are story continuity, not statements
about real people, organizations or current public transport.

## Start

Restart the application and load **Limmat — The Forbidden River** from the
campaign picker. Its installed path is `campaigns/limmat_forbidden_river`.
Installing the bundled source never overwrites an existing campaign folder or
loads it over your active world automatically.

Select **Field Arrival** for the protagonist's main story. **Campus Watch** has a
different PC at the safehouse. Its position, stats and private memory remain
separate, while the river seal, event history and public facts are shared.

See the [GM guide](GM_GUIDE.md) for the complete route, rule catalogue and feature
checks. The authored adventure has 27 scenes, 19 locations, 22 passages, 26
triggers, eight dynamic-state definitions, nine knowledge sources and three
mutually exclusive repair outcomes.

## Included files

- `campaign.json`: scenes, options, people, teams, rules, initial states and
  source metadata. It contains no model vectors or preplayed outcome history.
- `map.json`: the original 16-location topology translated and corrected for
  floor transitions, plus Li's Home, the River Archive and the Seal Chamber.
- `knowledge/`: editable English Markdown sources. Metadata in `rag_library`
  points to these files; their contents are read fresh on loading the folder.
- `GM_GUIDE.md`: operational walkthrough, trigger conditions and expected effects.

## Continuity

Hou Jiale remains president; Xu Sijie and Yu Zhihe remain vice presidents;
Zhao Yifei remains secretary-general. Gao Xing leads the Secretariat. Jia Weimeng
and Liu Jiao retain Public Relations; Zhang Dingyuan and Chen Yuyang retain
Corporate Relations; Jia Chu and Liu Runpu retain Culture and Sports; Zhang Xindi
and Huang Sice retain Publicity; Liu Zisen and Shang Haoliang retain Information.
Li remains the independent former ETH student, ACSSZ co-founder and planar master.

The new investigation gives these existing people scene roles without replacing
their offices. Li's true origin and a supposed culprit remain unestablished.
