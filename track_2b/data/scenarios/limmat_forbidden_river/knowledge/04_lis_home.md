# Li's Home — Freiestrasse 14

Li, an ACSSZ co-founder and former ETH student of unknown planar origin, created this neutral safehouse. It is protected from the two universities' detection and purging authority inside the ward. It is not a portable shield that follows a party outside.

The sofa restores body and mind; the bar mixes backlash remedies; colored ambient lights stabilize the local aether field; the sound system counters hostile logical noise and flesh-whispers. The balcony permits protected observation. The antique mirror may connect to Li's unknown home plane; what lies beyond remains unconfirmed.

Demo implementation: a deliberate stay in the Rest scene restores up to 8 HP and 8 SAN, capped at 100 each to its own party, with a real-time 60-second cooldown and at most two activations. It is blocked by irreversible logic deadlock and does not revive the dead, cure every state, or grant arbitrary maximum HP. A separate one-use calming tonic restores up to 5 SAN, capped at 100 after the player explicitly drinks it.
