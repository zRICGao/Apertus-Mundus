# Field assignment

The Limmat has begun reflecting two incompatible versions of the same dawn. ACSSZ wants evidence, civilian protection and a stable river seal. Collect the treaty appendix in the River Archive, a Calibration core from Liu Zisen's authorized lesson, and a Resonance vial from Xu Sijie's controlled exercise. These three concrete items unlock the three finale proposals.

Gao Xing provides a handbook, a Rune coin and a Midnight ticket during orientation. The coin opens the optional maintenance route through the map's explicit unlock action. It must not also be removed by a spell branch for the same unlock. ETH and UZH access credentials are earned through their respective lessons.

The second party, Campus Watch, starts at Li's Home with the Second Investigator. It shares the world but not the protagonist's draft, position or private memories. Send reports through confirmed events and merge parties only through the party tool.
