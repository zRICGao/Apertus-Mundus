# ACSSZ: people and responsibilities

ACSSZ is the Zurich Chinese Students and Scholars Association in the source story. Its ordinary student services conceal a neutral, cross-faction network protecting students caught between Die ETH and Die UZH. Orientation events, academic forums, recruitment and parties provide cover for support, supplies, diplomacy and rescue.

## Li — Founder and planar master

A former ETH student and ACSSZ co-founder who belongs to neither magical faction. His origins remain unknown. He created the neutral wards of the apartment at Freiestrasse 14. Do not reveal his home plane as established fact.

## Hou Jiale — President and supreme arbitrator

Coordinates ACSSZ survival strategy and arbitrates difficult decisions under the treaty. Leads the presidium without surrendering its neutrality.

## Xu Sijie — Vice president

Supports Hou Jiale in maintaining the underground network and its fragile peace. In this adventure, supervises the controlled vitality demonstration and records its cost.

## Yu Zhihe — Vice president

Supports Hou Jiale in maintaining the underground network and its fragile peace. Coordinates the safehouse watch in this adventure.

## Zhao Yifei — Secretary-general and confidential dispatcher

Runs confidential dispatch for the presidium. Receives reports from both field parties and keeps requests attributable to their actual source.

## Gao Xing — Head of the Secretariat / Privy Council

Uses orientation and newcomer support as cover for guiding new awakeners, erasing magical traces and protecting mundane memories. Welcomes the protagonist at HB.

## Jia Weimeng — Head of Public Relations / Aether Negotiation Bureau

Handles dangerous diplomacy with the two universities and contains aether incidents. Can broker the neutral river-seal agreement, but cannot promise consent from an absent faction.

## Liu Jiao — Public Relations deputy

Assists Jia Weimeng with faction diplomacy and public-safety cover. Knows which apparent tourists are watching Central.

## Zhang Dingyuan — Head of Corporate Relations / underground supplies

Organizes sponsorship as cover for acquiring and allocating scarce restoration supplies and magical materials. Keeps a strict inventory.

## Chen Yuyang — Corporate Relations deputy

Assists Zhang Dingyuan with cross-faction supplies. Provides one documented calming tonic instead of unlimited free healing.

## Jia Chu — Head of Culture and Sports / tactical rescue

Converts public events into defensive rituals and coordinates extraction from dangerous magical areas. Does not teleport teams through locked passages.

## Liu Runpu — Culture and Sports deputy

Assists Jia Chu with group defense and tactical rescue under ordinary crowd cover.

## Zhang Xindi — Head of Publicity / Illusion Weaving Bureau

Uses media cover and cognitive interference to disguise supernatural incidents as campus activities or natural phenomena.

## Huang Sice — Publicity deputy

Assists Zhang Xindi with illusion weaving and public explanations. Distinguishes an approved cover story from a confirmed world fact.

## Liu Zisen — Head of Information / cyber-ward infrastructure

Builds digital wards, communications and counter-tracking defenses. In this adventure, teaches the authorized geometric calibration and documents its SAN cost.

## Shang Haoliang — Information deputy

Assists Liu Zisen with communications, digital wards and counter-tracking. Can compare the two field parties' authenticated observations.

These are the source campaign's characters, not replacement characters. The magical roles and events belong to this fictional setting. Original names are preserved as entity aliases.
