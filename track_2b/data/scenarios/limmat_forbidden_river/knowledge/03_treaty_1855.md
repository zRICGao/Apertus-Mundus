# The 1855 Limmat Treaty

The pact between Die ETH and Die UZH makes concealment from ordinary citizens an absolute obligation. Its enforcers claim reciprocal purging authority against uncontrolled public magic. HB and Central are particularly exposed. A spell merely proposed, imagined, quoted or observed in a document is not a committed public breach.

ACSSZ attempts concealment and evacuation rather than open battle. The technical demo records an exposure flag and an explicit warning; it does not silently kill a character. A GM decides any subsequent pursuit through normal validated actions. Li's Home at Freiestrasse 14 is protected by a separate neutral planar ward and exempt from faction surveillance inside its boundary.

A newly recovered appendix describes a joint river seal. A geometric calibration and a living resonance are complementary inputs, not proof that either university alone caused the current anomaly.
