# The joint seal

The Treaty copy identifies a two-channel seal. The Calibration core records the geometric phase. The Resonance vial carries the living phase. Comparing them shows a timing mismatch rather than sabotage established by either faction. No document identifies a guilty individual.

At the Seal Chamber, choose one intervention: ETH computation costs 10 SAN; UZH vitality costs 10 HP; a neutral joint procedure costs 5 HP and 5 SAN. Each consumes the two prepared components and sets the single shared seal outcome. The three outcomes exclude one another. A choice is only a proposal until committed.

The neutral procedure has Jia Weimeng negotiating a limited joint maintenance agreement, with Hou Jiale arbitrating and Zhao Yifei recording it. It does not end the 1855 treaty or solve every future dispute.
