# Polybahn: fictional folded services

This secret timetable belongs to the adventure, not to the real public railway. Stable departures occur at 00:05, 00:11, 00:17 and 00:23. The 00:23 Abyss Geek Line bypasses the surface aether layer for the deepest Polyhedral Core laboratories. The demo uses 00:11.

At door closure, an authorized quantum terminal or a computationally charged titanium compass initiates the fold. The source story's Hermes array returns ordinary accidental riders to Polyterrasse the next morning after erasing short-term memories. Unauthorized cross-faction riding risks overwhelming logical shock.

The fold lasts 114 fictional seconds. Do not look out: the river becomes a waterfall of exposed world-code. In the source rules, analyzing that view can empty SAN and cause irreversible logic deadlock. The authored walkthrough keeps the blinds shut. The dangerous act requires explicit player intent and GM handling; it is never inferred from looking at the map.

Demo admission: the scene's physical terminal is pre-charged by the guide; the player needs the handbook and Midnight ticket, consents to waiting for the fictional departure, pays 6 SAN once, and is moved by the hard trigger to the registered folded cabin. The ticket is consumed exactly once.
