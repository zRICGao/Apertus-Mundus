# Aether aberrations

Leaked UZH vitality may produce aggressive flesh growth in animals or plants; attacks harm HP. ETH computational waste may produce silicon and geometric distortions in inorganic matter or insects; logical noise harms SAN. Materials recovered from these hazards can be useful, but a name alone does not establish a creature's identity or threat level.

At grade three or higher, withdraw and contact support instead of fighting alone. Jia Chu and Liu Runpu coordinate rescue; Liu Zisen and Shang Haoliang protect communications. Physical contact, wounds and costs must be committed through the world engine. Mere proximity is not automatic repeated damage.
