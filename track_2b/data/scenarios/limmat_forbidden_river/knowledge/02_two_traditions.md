# The two traditions

Die ETH (Eidgenoessische Techno-Hermetische Hochschule) treats magic as advanced mathematics and physics. Rune-etched titanium compasses and disguised quantum terminals support exact geometric calculations. Casting consumes SAN; excessive computation causes logic deadlock, overload and hallucinations. The Polyhedral Core lies below the main building.

Die UZH (Universitas Zauberkunst Helvetica) treats magic as resonance among life, collective consciousness and natural law. Parchment and spirit-bearing fountain pens channel vitality. Casting consumes HP; excessive use causes weakness and mutation. Its hidden centers include the Verdant Temple near Irchelpark and the Central Library labyrinth.

No cost-free casting: a committed spell pays the appropriate cost exactly once. A trigger must not charge again for a cost already included in a generated branch. Studying a diagram or discussing magic is not itself a cast. Neither tradition determines a student's identity automatically.
