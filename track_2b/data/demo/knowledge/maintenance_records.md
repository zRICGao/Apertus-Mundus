# Lighthouse maintenance records

The lighthouse console is in the foyer. The lighting equipment is in the upstairs lantern room. Keeper Wen Xi holds the Lantern room key.

Two short alarms indicate a power circuit fault; they do not establish that the bulb is broken. Check the fuse in the foyer console before inspecting the lantern room equipment.

The toolkit can remove the console cover. Disconnect power before replacing the fuse. Spare fuses are in a small box inside the console cover.

All places, characters and equipment above are original fictional content created for this project demo.
